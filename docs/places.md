# Places and establishment

A Place is an existing location in the narrative. A Venue is a location admitted to village mechanics. Building a new structure still uses a New Venue Project. Saying that a new building exists cannot complete construction or supply Project resources.

Players can learn about Places through ordinary Scene exchanges, selected lore and explicit **Explore**. The current GM Scene exchange validates up to two grounded introductions per reply. Reports stay unverified. Known locations are reused; private knowledge remains individual. A single Explore action can find one suitable existing or new Place, or none. Blank input explores nearby; a creature or object alone is not a Place. First-visit preparation fills an unprepared location with up to six Zones besides Entrance and can associate up to four established Villagers, Visitors or NPCs. It cannot invent people from a location name. A failed or unusable Place generation retries once automatically, then quietly returns to idle. The player has no saved Place request, Retry or Dismiss controls. Internal receipts prevent duplicate calls and publication; refresh and browsing do not generate replacements.

Places reuse Scene entry, Zone navigation, connections, access, acoustic witnesses and playback. NPC associations do not imply presence. An observed arrival must be proposed in the current GM exchange and validated against admitted positions; it cannot hear earlier conversation. Supporting characters retain compact identities, scoped associations, availability and native Memories. They have no Villager relationship scores, Agendas, autonomous pursuit, employment or construction leadership. Their native Memories can retain motives, desires and commitments. A reply has at most four responding people. NPCs and the player consume no Villager occupancy slots.

Maps do not generate a starting NPC population. Founding, Scene entry and browsing People make no NPC-population request. Previously established NPCs and saved population receipts remain readable.

The GM can improvise at most one new individual per response. An accepted witnessed encounter atomically retains that person's stable identity, appearance, characterization and native Memories with its Episode. Anonymous crowds and mere mentions need no character records. New encounters are unlisted in People by default; visibility can be changed deliberately through REPAIR. A role label remains visible until the player hears that person's canonical name. Appearance is shown only after an encounter.

Server-side name, Map, Place and text indexes select a small set of relevant NPCs before generation. Scene candidate profiles and identity references are bounded; NPC Memories use the shared local recall budget. Saved identities and history can grow with play without becoming a whole-world prompt or background simulation. Bulk records remain in the existing immutable history pages; cold reads still hydrate retained history. No separate NPC director, periodic biography rewrite or management model call is used. New names cannot copy established identities or shortened names. A Place's established controller or resident remains that person when absent; its name does not create a substitute or grant authority.

A Place can host ordinary activity and retain supported nonstructural changes to its rooms. Official Venue functions and structural changes require Projects. Controllers can issue entry permission or consent within their recorded scope. Authority comes from the saved controller record.

**Establish as Venue** creates a Project for an ongoing village use, selected spaces, agreements and up to eight open-ended objectives. The current GM Scene exchange can cite witnessed outcomes for those exact objectives; promises do not manufacture completed physical results. There is no separate quest interpreter or model polling. Social establishment has no compulsory lead, work focus or construction timer: Start reserves capacity and Complete establishes it once the recorded requirements are met. A physical establishment plan reuses Renovation design, a willing Villager lead, six hours of lead focus and the existing 24 elapsed-hour construction lifecycle. Prerequisites and consent must be resolved before physical Start. Only affected Zones close; cancellation leaves the Place available.

Promotion keeps the same location, Zone and supporting-character IDs, access and history. It adds eligibility for ordinary Venue mechanics without assigning ownership, jobs or housing, revealing private interiors or resetting conditions. Later Renovations still need scoped controllers' agreements.

The existing limits remain: 16 Venues across the whole Village, two active Projects (including paused work), and six drafts. Places consume no Venue slot. Planning reserves no Venue slot; an active establishment reserves one, counted once through completion. No gameplay cap is imposed on stored Places. The directory returns 50 records per page; the optional map layer requests at most 100 markers within its viewport. Prompts receive bounded relevant summaries, never the entire Place library.

Private Place/NPC records and source receipts use the existing immutable history pages and Village CAS. Every NPC, including unlisted encounters, is inspectable through REPAIR. Its identity, appearance, Map, directory visibility, location associations, availability and disabled state can be corrected; Memories use the existing correction/Forget controls. Corrections use reviewed changes, Undo and checkpoints. Disabling a record stops its future use. Existing Scenes retain their admitted sources and witnesses; identity edits do not rewrite played dialogue or Memories.

The current build requires a fresh format-19 Village. Older saves are rejected and their storage remains intact.
