# Places and establishment

A Place is an existing location in the narrative. A Venue is a location admitted to village mechanics. Building a new structure still uses a New Venue Project. Saying that a new building exists cannot complete construction or supply Project resources.

Players can learn about Places through ordinary Scene exchanges, selected lore and explicit **Explore**. The existing System exchange validates up to two grounded introductions per reply. Reports stay unverified. Known locations are reused; private knowledge remains individual. A single Explore action can find one suitable existing or new Place, or none. Blank input explores nearby; a creature or object alone is not a Place. First-visit preparation fills an unprepared location with up to six Zones besides Entrance and up to four relevant supporting identities. A failed or unusable generation retries once automatically, then quietly returns to idle. The player has no saved request, Retry or Dismiss controls. Internal receipts prevent duplicate calls and publication; refresh and browsing do not generate replacements.

Places reuse Scene entry, Zone navigation, connections, access, acoustic witnesses and playback. NPC associations do not imply presence. An observed arrival must be approved in the existing System exchange; it cannot hear earlier conversation. Supporting characters retain compact identities, scoped associations, availability and native Memories. They have no Villager relationship scores, Agendas, autonomous activity, employment, Wishes or construction leadership. A reply has at most four responding people. NPCs and the player consume no Villager occupancy slots.

A Place can host ordinary activity and temporary details. Enduring alterations and normal village management require establishment. Explicitly established controllers can issue entry permission or consent only within their recorded scope; a job title grants no authority.

**Establish as Venue** creates a Project for an ongoing village use, selected spaces, agreements and up to eight open-ended objectives. The current System exchange can cite witnessed outcomes for those exact objectives; promises do not manufacture completed physical results. There is no separate quest interpreter or model polling. Social establishment has no compulsory lead, work focus or construction timer: Start reserves capacity and Complete establishes it once the recorded requirements are met. A physical establishment plan reuses Renovation design, a willing Villager lead, six hours of lead focus and the existing 24 elapsed-hour construction lifecycle. Prerequisites and consent must be resolved before physical Start. Only affected Zones close; cancellation leaves the Place available.

Promotion keeps the same location, Zone and supporting-character IDs, access and history. It adds eligibility for ordinary Venue mechanics without assigning ownership, jobs or housing, revealing private interiors or resetting conditions. Later Renovations still need scoped controllers' agreements.

The existing limits remain: 16 Venues across the whole Village, two active Projects (including paused work), and six drafts. Places consume no Venue slot. Planning reserves no Venue slot; an active establishment reserves one, counted once through completion. No gameplay cap is imposed on stored Places. The directory returns 50 records per page; the optional map layer requests at most 100 markers within its viewport. Prompts receive bounded relevant summaries, never the entire Place library.

Private Place/NPC records and source receipts use the existing immutable history pages and Village CAS. Supporting identity/availability, Place records and Memories are inspectable through REPAIR; corrections use its review, Undo and checkpoint flow. Disabling a record stops its future use. Existing Scenes retain their admitted sources and witnesses.

Version 0.8.0 requires a fresh format-16 Village. Old-save conversion is intentionally omitted by the owner's explicit decision for this unreleased single-tester build. Installing the package does not erase other environments or reset their storage.
