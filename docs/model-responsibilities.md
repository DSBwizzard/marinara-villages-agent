# Model responsibilities

The Scene GM writes and interprets one exchange in one response using the saved Narration connection and model. Code validates exact sources, witnesses, access, revisions and receipts before effects commit. See [GM ownership](system-ownership.md) and the [generation lifecycle](generation-pipeline.md).

Relationships remain qualitative resident-owned perspectives in common continuity. The player authors their own feelings and consent. Captured holder revisions and current protection checks prevent stale effects from restoring superseded terms.

Starting social history is finite System preparation from exact authored cards, player backstory and selected lore. Complete required identity must fit unchanged. Contradictions ask for targeted creator clarification; later admissions and card refreshes preserve played developments.

Personal replies combine correspondence and its source/reply effects in one GM response. Resident-origin mail and explicit interpretation of retained correspondence use their own System task. Exact outgoing words save before dispatch, original delivery settles before reply effects, and unread disclosures remain outside the notebook. Reading and local replay call no model.

Resident encounters and world developments each return content and proposed effects together. Scheduling and planning retain their separate bounded admissions. World developments receive an immutable grant for one existing prepared Zone and separate timed weather authority. The grant covers only the supplied circumstances. Consequential Room edits and conditions settle together through current Village authority. A change in observed appearance can mark artwork out of date without queuing an image request.

Automatic resident/world activity shares the configured daily allowance and live spacing. Failed, empty and unknown attempts consume their admission. Absence catch-up retains its captured budget date and opportunity ceiling; it cannot borrow a later day's allowance. Held work is not automatically replaced. Explicit reinterpretation keeps the original source words, and local replay reuses its saved response.

## Context and cost boundaries

Current writing requests capture their contract, exact source and eligibility at admission. Unsupported contracts fail before provider work. Only complete normally stopped JSON can receive current sparse defaults; malformed supplied effects, duplicate keys and truncation remain errors.

Consequential proposals cite generated segment labels or fitted evidence. Grounded input references bind exact submitted text and occurrences. Code checks the eligible witnesses, scope and settlement requirements for the proposed effect.

A movement command needs a different destination, current admission and an observed crossing. Code validates the route, access and capacity at the captured exchange time. Physical positions at that segment determine subsequent witnesses and staging.

Authored cards, instructions and prose preferences remain required. Local selection gives recent shared Episodes priority and ranks older history by textual relevance. All Episode recall is optional during fitting. Only fitted evidence can support effects, and explicit character perceptions define who can know each excerpt. See [Memories](memories-core.md).

Reservations, failures, explicit retries and background tasks all contribute to play cost. Prompt organization does not establish provider-cache hits or savings. Provider-free tests establish routing, limits and accounting; real-provider characterization, latency, protocol quality and cost require separately authorized observation.
